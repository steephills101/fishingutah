What Is Space?

Space is the vast region beyond Earth's atmosphere. It contains planets, stars, galaxies, moons, asteroids, comets, and many other objects. Scientists study space to better understand the origins and evolution of our universe.

🌌 Galaxies

Galaxies are enormous collections of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.

⭐ Stars

Stars are massive balls of extremely hot plasma that produce energy through nuclear fusion.

🪐 Planets

Planets orbit stars and can have very different sizes, temperatures, atmospheres, and compositions.

Amazing Objects in Space

The universe contains objects that are both enormous and incredibly strange. Here are a few of the most interesting.

🕳️ Black Holes

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is so strong that nothing, including light, can escape once it passes the event horizon.

☄️ Comets

Comets are icy objects that travel through space. When they approach the Sun, they can develop glowing comas and tails.

🌑 Moons

Moons are natural satellites that orbit planets and other celestial bodies.
