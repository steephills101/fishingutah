Fishing in Utah

Utah offers fishing opportunities throughout the year. Anglers can fish in mountain streams, rivers, lakes, reservoirs and community ponds across the state.

🏔️ Mountain Fishing

Utah's mountains contain streams and lakes where anglers can pursue trout and other fish in scenic surroundings.

🏜️ Desert Waters

Southern Utah offers a very different fishing environment, including large reservoirs surrounded by red-rock scenery.

❄️ Ice Fishing

During winter, some Utah anglers fish through the ice on suitable frozen lakes and reservoirs.

🎣 Year-Round Fishing

Utah DWR describes fishing in Utah as a year-round activity, with opportunities varying by location and season.

Fish You Can Find in Utah

Utah waters contain many different sportfish and native fish species.

🌈 Rainbow Trout

Rainbow trout are a popular sportfish and are found in many Utah fisheries.

🐟 Cutthroat Trout

Cutthroat trout are especially important to Utah's native fish heritage and include several subspecies.

🐠 Brown Trout

Brown trout are another popular trout species found in Utah waters.

🐟 Kokanee Salmon

Kokanee salmon are a landlocked form of sockeye salmon and are known for their striking appearance.

🐡 Largemouth Bass

Largemouth bass are a popular warm-water sportfish found in several Utah reservoirs.

🐟 Smallmouth Bass

Smallmouth bass are known for their strong fights and are pursued by anglers in Utah.

🐟 Walleye

Walleye are found in several Utah fisheries and are popular with anglers.

🐱 Channel Catfish

Channel catfish are another popular species and can grow to impressive sizes.

Popular Utah Fishing Waters

Utah has a wide variety of places to fish, from large reservoirs to mountain streams.

🏞️ Lake Powell

Lake Powell is famous for its massive size, spectacular canyon scenery and variety of fishing opportunities. Utah's state-record striped bass was caught here.

🎣 Flaming Gorge Reservoir

Flaming Gorge is a major Utah fishing destination known for trout, kokanee salmon and other species.

🏔️ Fish Lake

Fish Lake is a well-known mountain fishing destination in central Utah.

🌊 Utah Lake

Utah Lake provides fishing opportunities near one of Utah's largest population centers.

Utah Fishing Records

Utah maintains official records for fish caught using different legal fishing methods.

🏆 Striped Bass

Utah's catch-and-keep striped bass record is 48 lb 11 oz.

🏆 Largemouth Bass

Utah's catch-and-keep largemouth bass record is 10 lb 2 oz.

🏆 Smallmouth Bass

Utah's catch-and-keep smallmouth bass record is 7 lb 6 oz.

🏆 Bluegill

A Utah bluegill record of 2 lb 8 oz was established in 2024 at Quail Creek Reservoir.

Fishing Tips for Beginners

Getting started with fishing doesn't have to be complicated.

  • Get the proper Utah fishing license if required for your age.
  • Check the regulations for the specific lake, river or reservoir before fishing.
  • Bring appropriate tackle, bait or lures for the species you are targeting.
  • Carry water, sunscreen, food and weather-appropriate clothing.
  • Handle fish carefully if you plan to release them.
  • Respect wildlife and leave the fishing area cleaner than you found it.
  • Check current fishing conditions before leaving home.

Know the Rules

⚠️ Always Check Current Regulations

Utah fishing regulations can vary by species and by individual waterbody. Daily limits, fishing methods and special regulations can change. The official Utah Fishing Guidebook is the best source to check before your fishing trip.

Fishing Licenses

Utah requires fishing licenses for anglers who meet the applicable age requirements. According to Utah DWR, people under 12 can fish without a fishing license, while anglers 12 and older generally need one.
